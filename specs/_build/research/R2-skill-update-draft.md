## 4. Delivery notes for Phase 6

- These tables are the proposal body. Pierce owns whether the skill is edited;
  nothing here is applied by the research or implementation waves.
- Items **1, 2, 3, 5, 6** are the ones that make the skill's *own* templates
  produce a repo that cannot build or version itself correctly. If the proposal
  is trimmed, keep those five.
- Items **5, 6, 9** are the ADR-driven ones (ADR-0007, ADR-0006 §3, ADR-0002)
  and should be presented together, since they are one story: *stop putting
  build-varying values where a generator has to patch them.*
- Every claim above is reproducible from the file:line citations plus
  `R2-toolchain.md` §Sources.
